As part of my interest in sustainability and engineering, I became a member of Coastalong's Engineering Committee. Coastalong is a bike-powered music festival hosted annually at UCLA, focused on producing zero waste and hosting a free artist. Students pedal bikes to generate the power for the musical guest. Using 100% second-hand materials, I designed a bubble-blowing bike for the festival. Festival-goers could pedal around the field and the rotation of a wheel on the bike would generate bubbles. This was a fun way explore engineering when the main goal is sustainability and a key constraint is available resources. 

I've noticed that in a lot of engineering spaces, we tend to think of what the user is going to do with a product, but less about the waste we're creating along the way. As an engineer, I hope to be more mindful about my carbon footprint and build more sustainably.
